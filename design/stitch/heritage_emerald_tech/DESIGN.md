## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an identity around reliable, community-rooted commerce combined with elite tech craftsmanship. Designed for a high-volume mobile trade-in, refurbishment, and expert repair hub, the aesthetic marries the prestige and goodwill of an established local institution with the speed, clarity, and precision of tier-one modern recommerce platforms.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** The interface conveys institutional credibility and fair pricing transparency, dissolving customer friction regarding repair integrity and device appraisal.
- **Warm Prestige:** Moving far away from clinical, cold tech-blue templates, the presence of warm ivory, emerald green, and champagne gold communicates bespoke care, value, and stability.
- **Accessible Sophistication:** Built for a diverse demographic across Tier-2/Tier-3 tech ecosystems, every interaction feels immediately familiar, legible, and crisp while retaining high-end polish.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Warm Modernism** with **Tactile Utility**. Surfaces rely on layered, ultra-clean off-white containers framed by hairline chamfered borders. Elevation avoids artificial neon glows, favoring sun-lit olive-tinted micro-shadows, structured brand grids, and high-clarity transactional modules.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable sense of heritage and precision, drawing from organic mineral deep greens, ceremonial golds, and soothing linen undertones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Emerald Suite (`#163A24`, `#1B432C`, `#0F2919`):** Represents institutional authority, device security, and technological mastery. Anchors main action buttons, major category headers, and active state highlights.
- **Warm Gold & Champagne Accents (`#C59B27`, `#D4AF37`, `#F9F4E8`):** Applied selectively to warranty badges, diagnostic certifications, trade-in bonus indicators, and premium Grade-A badges.
- **Utility / Action (`#25D366`):** Dedicated to high-conversion direct assistance channels, notably frictionless WhatsApp instant diagnostics and support.
- **Surfaces & Canvases (`#FAF8F5`, `#F4EFEA`, `#FFFFFF`):** The default application backdrop sits on warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`), with higher-density interactive elements housed in crisp `#FFFFFF` cards, framed gently by subtle structural borders (`#E5DFD5`).
- **Slate Foliage Neutrals (`#2C3E35`, `#4B5E53`, `#88988E`):** Pure black is strictly avoided. High-contrast body typography relies on deep slate evergreen (`#2C3E35`), preserving eye comfort under prolonged sunlight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered around lightning-fast scanning, numerical transparency, and cross-generational readability.

### Type Pairings
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings contemporary warmth and geometric rhythm. High-impact weights (`700`, `800`) are used for valuation quotes, service headlines, and product tier headings.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Serves as the functional workhorse. Inter's tall x-height, clear apertures, and distinct numeral forms make instant price comparisons, device serial numbers, and condition reports immediately legible, even on budget displays in bright sunlight.

### Numerical & Currency Formatting
All Rupee (`₹`) pricing and condition metrics must utilize tabular figure styles (`tnum`) with medium-to-bold weights to prevent layout shifts during trade-in valuation adjustments.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop breakpoints (`≥ 1024px`), transforming to a 6-column grid on tablets (`≥ 640px`) and an agile 4-column layout on mobile devices (`< 640px`).

### Rhythms and Structure
- **Screen Margins:** Fixed at `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports to maximize usable card surface for brand grids and repair lists, transitioning to `2rem` (32px) on larger screens with a capped max content container of `1240px`.
- **Card Spacing:** Dense 8px grid increments dominate component internals (`space-xs` through `space-md`), keeping multi-step appraisal diagnostic trees tight, compact, and scannable without requiring excessive vertical scrolling.
- **Touch Targets:** Any interactive button, brand selector pill, or diagnosis checkbox adheres to an uncompromising minimum hit target of `48px × 48px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic drop shadows, adopting organic, warm, ambient occlusion reminiscent of natural daylight on linen and glass.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF8F5` canvas base.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline stroke (`1px solid #E5DFD5`) paired with a feathered ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 58, 36, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(44, 62, 53, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Hover):** `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(22, 58, 36, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(22, 58, 36, 0.03)`. Used for hovered refurbished phone cards and diagnostic price summaries.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & WhatsApp Trigger):** `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 41, 25, 0.16)`. Applied to sticky bottom valuation ribbons and floating contact modules.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** philosophy. The core corner radius sits at `0.5rem` (8px), scaling to `1rem` (16px) for larger surface containers and cards, and fully circular pill forms for trust chips and status badges.

### Shape Rationale
- **Cards & Steppers (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Delivers a comfortable handheld feel, preventing clinical sharp corners while avoiding toy-like over-rounding.
- **Brand Selectors & Grade Pills (`rounded-full`):** Retains immediate affordance as clickable, selectable chips.
- **Input Fields & Action Controls (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Balances modern tactile responsiveness with structural neatness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Forest Green (`#163A24`) fill with clean white text (`#FFFFFF`), `12px 24px` padding, `8px` corner radius. Interactive state shifts to `#0F2919` with a subtle inward glow.
- **Secondary CTA:** Cream Ivory surface (`#F4EFEA`) with Emerald outline (`1.5px solid #163A24`) and green text.
- **WhatsApp Support Trigger:** Floating circular or pill button styled with `#25D366` background, crisp white typography/icon, anchored at bottom-right with elevation Level 3.

### Grade Tags & Trust Badges
- **Grade A (Superb):** Deep emerald badge (`#163A24`) with warm gold lettering (`#D4AF37`) and subtle champagne border (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Grade B (Good Value):** Muted sage fill (`#E8EFEA`) with deep forest text (`#1B432C`).
- **Grade C (Budget):** Neutral canvas fill (`#F4EFEA`) with slate neutral text (`#4B5E53`).
- **Trust Badges:** Compact inline chips with miniature gold shield icons featuring text such as "6-Month Warranty", "32-Point Check Passed", or "Genuine Parts Assured".

### Brand Selector Grids
- 3 to 4 column mobile layouts featuring square or 4:3 ratio white cards bordered by `#E5DFD5`.
- Selected state displays an active `2px` ring in `#163A24` accompanied by a micro champagne checkmark badge at the top right.

### Valuation & Pricing Steppers
- Segmented progression bars displaying real-time calculation changes.
- Deductions and condition toggles are housed in tactile radio/switch cells with smooth background transitions from `#FFFFFF` to light emerald tint (`#F1F6F3`) when toggled active.

### Refurbished Product Cards
- Image container with neutral off-white background (`#F9F7F4`), device photo, floating Grade Tag at top left, and trade-in discount pill at top right.
- Explicit visual pricing hierarchy: Final offer price in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`#163A24`), strikethrough original MRP in muted slate (`#88988E`), followed by warranty micro-copy.
